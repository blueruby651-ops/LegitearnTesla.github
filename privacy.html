Privacy Policy

Last updated: November 2026

1. Who we are

LegitEarn runs this website. This page explains what personal information we collect and how we use it.

2. What we collect

  • Your name and email address.
  • Your password. It is stored securely by our login provider, and we cannot see it.
  • Your task answers, rewards and withdrawal history.
  • Your bank name, account name and account number, so we can pay you.

3. Why we use it

  • To run your account and show your balance.
  • To review tasks and pay rewards and withdrawals.
  • To prevent fraud and misuse.
  • To keep financial records as the law may require.

4. Who can see it

Only you and our authorised administrators can see your account information, and only as needed to run the service. Our technology providers, such as the database host and email sender, process data for us. We do not sell your information.

5. How long we keep it

We keep your information while your account is active. We keep financial records for as long as the law or our accounting needs require.

6. Your rights

Under Nigerian data protection law you can ask to see, correct or delete your information, and you can complain to the Nigeria Data Protection Commission. Contact us to make a request.

7. Security

We use encrypted connections and access controls to protect your information. No online service is completely risk free, so please keep your password private.

8. Children

LegitEarn is for people aged 18 and over.

9. Changes

We may update this policy and will change the date above when we do.
